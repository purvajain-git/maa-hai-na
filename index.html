Our Story

A promise, before it was a brand.

“Maa hai naa” is what she says when things feel like too much. It means, simply, mother is here. Not a slogan — a promise she's made a hundred times, in a hundred small ways.

This brand began the way most home remedies do: in a kitchen, with whatever was already on the shelf, and a mother who knew exactly what to do with it. Oil warmed between her palms before it touched your scalp. Powders mixed fresh, not bought ready-made. A halwa put together the night a baby arrived, because someone remembered it needed making.

rooted in care, made for you

What She Knew

The oils, powders, and mixes lived in her hands and her memory, made fresh each time, never quite the same way twice.

What She Passed On

Recipes taught by doing, not writing down — watched over a shoulder, corrected by touch and smell.

What We Made Simple

The same knowledge, put into a jar you can reach for on an ordinary day — nothing added that wasn't already there.

There is no version of care older than a mother's hands. We just made it easier to reach.
